Catan

Catan keeps its own board inside Jackdaw's frame: the classic island of hexes, cream number discs with red sixes and eights, ships at the harbours and the four classic piece colours, printed in Jackdaw's card-stock style. Pick a board look first. Everything below it redraws in the look you choose.

3 Â· Phones

Playing on a phone

The board always fits the width, with no zooming needed on the classic board. You can only tap the spots where a move is legal, and a tap places a ghost piece first, so a fat thumb never builds in the wrong place. Trading and discarding open as sheets over the board.

Your turn, after the roll

The roll lands, the producing tiles light up and what you got rises over your hand. Others show their cards, development cards, points and awards.

Building a settlement

Only corners you can build on are ringed. Tap one to see the ghost; Build here pays and places it.

Making an offer

You give, you want, to everyone or one player. Answers come in live; you pick who to trade with. Bank and harbour trades are a row below.

An offer to you

On someone else's turn you can accept, counter or decline, and propose your own trade to them.

Ships bound for the island

Catan uses Jackdaw's lobby (code, options, chat and Start stay where everyone knows them) with its own seats, as Risk has its war council. 6 Â· How it plays

Rules and moments, in short

Rules

  • Official 5th-edition rules: snake setup, the robber and discarding on a 7, harbours, development cards, Longest Road and Largest Army, first to 10 on your own turn.
  • Five and six players get the big island and catan.com's paired turns: a second player builds alongside each turn.
  • Trade and build in any order (the rulebook's combined phase).

Results you can feel

  • Each roll: what you got rises over your hand. Nothing on a roll gives a quiet line.
  • Robbed: a gules moment ("Rook took a card"). The thief gets a vert one.
  • Longest Road or Largest Army won or lost: a moment for both players.
  • The end: everyone's Verdict, the final board and how each player made their points.

Options (proposed)

  • Board: Balanced (default), Beginner, Random.
  • Points to win: 10 (default), 8 for a short night, 12.
  • Friendly robber: off by default.
  • Turn timer: off, 1, 2 or 3 minutes.
What I need from you

Three choices

  1. Board look: A (my pick), B or C, or a mix (for example A's colours with C's quieter tiles in dark mode).

  2. Lobby and TV: do the ships at the harbours feel right for Catan's seats, or would you rather have something else, such as a settlement per seat?

  3. Anything on the phone that feels crowded or missing: the hand, the four buttons, the other players' chips.
